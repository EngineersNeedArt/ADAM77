SLOtS (a slot machine game)
----------------------------------------
Start: 0200 G

Instructions: Press a button to spin 
  the reels. 1 credit cost to spin. GO 
  starts a new game if you lose all your
  credits.

Symbols:
  seven (7),
  bar (-), 
  double-toothpicks (||)
  cherry (d)

        7    7    7    pays 50
       BAR  BAR  BAR   pays 15
       ||   ||   ||    pays  5
        d    d    d    pays  4
        d    d    any  pays  2
        d    any  any  pays  1
