YAHt-C (a dice game)
----------------------------------------
Start: 0200 G

GO starts a game.
You have three chances to roll five dice
to score. After first roll, toggle which
dice to re-roll. The dice are numbered
(1-5). A flashing die will be re-rolled.
Press DA to re-roll.

After the 2nd re-roll, your dice will be
scored. Press AD any time to score the 
dice without re-rolling.

When the game ends your final score is 
displayed. Press GO to start a new game.
