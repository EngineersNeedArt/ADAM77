Asteroid (Stan Ockers)
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
0200 G
––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––––
Description: You are piloting your 
spacecraft between Mars & Jupiter when 
you encounter a dense portion of the 
asteroid belt. Press key “0” to move 
left, “3” to move right. When your craft
is hit the display will give a number to
indicate how successful you were.
